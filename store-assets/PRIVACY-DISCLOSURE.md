# Store privacy disclosure

- Flowloud does not sell data, use data for advertising, or collect analytics.
- Website content and digital PDF text are handled locally unless the user chooses an online TTS, OCR, or translation provider.
- Local-service text is sent only to a user-configured loopback address (`localhost`, `127.0.0.1`, or `::1`).
- Authentication information is used only to call the provider configured by the user. Online keys and local tokens are session-only unless their separate “Remember” option is explicitly selected.
- Images, visible-tab screenshots, and rendered scanned-PDF pages are transferred only after the user selects a Profile, grants the exact API origin, and confirms that session's upload. Workbench documents and results are not persisted.
- Raw images, document text, reference audio, full API responses, and credentials are excluded from logs and diagnostics.
- Audio samples are sent to Microsoft Edge speech recognition only after a separate disclosure and affirmative consent; manual transcription remains available.
- Data is not used for unrelated purposes and is not transferred to data brokers.
- The published privacy policy must match `docs/privacy.md` and the final GitHub Pages URL must be entered in both store dashboards.
